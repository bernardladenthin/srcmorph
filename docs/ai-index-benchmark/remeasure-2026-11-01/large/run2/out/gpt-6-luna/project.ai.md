#### Overview
The project combines stable, categorized platform error identifiers and messages with a versioned product catalog covering EUR pricing, warehouse inventory, and supplier recalls. Its SQL ledger stores customer and bank-account records, account-to-account transfers, daily transfer totals, and fraud-review holds.
