#### Overview
The project centers on a unified toolkit that processes web‑server logs, automates deployment and health monitoring of a payment service, and manages billing documents. Log parsing and aggregation are handled by Perl and shell utilities, while XML infrastructure supports invoicing, reminders, shipment validation, and statement rendering. Together these subsystems provide end‑to‑end support for payment processing, monitoring, and billing workflows.
