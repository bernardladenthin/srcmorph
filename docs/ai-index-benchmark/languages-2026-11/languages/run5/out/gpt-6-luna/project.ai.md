#### Overview
Billing and payment operations span access-log analysis, service deployment and queue-worker management, and settled-payment exports. The shell scripts handle deployment, worker control, and payment export; Perl tools turn web-server logs into request, host, status, and slow-request statistics, while XML configurations support invoice builds, overdue reminders, shipment validation, and account-statement rendering.
