#### Overview
The project centers on billing and service operations, with payment exports, invoice and reminder configuration, shipment validation, and account-statement rendering. Shell tools handle release rollouts, worker health checks, and nightly settled-payment exports, while Perl access-log reporting summarizes requests by host and status and highlights busy hosts and slow requests.
